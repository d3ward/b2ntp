A few key points to understand how b2ntp works and safeguards your bookmarks:

  • All data stays on your device. No information is sent to external servers
  • Your bookmarks remain untouched and secure as uses an offline local copy (clone) of your actual bookmarks
  • The extension cannot delete or edit your original bookmarks

          How it works
          As you get familiar with these tips, your searches will become quicker and more efficient!
          Bookmarks Highlight

          If your search term matches a bookmark, it will be highlighted automatically


          Search + Skip Bookmark

          If you don't want to open the highlighted bookmark, press the space bar twice, then Enter. This will perform a search using your default search engine.


          Search with Shortcut

          To use a shortcut, start by typing the bang+shortcut letter, followed by a space, and then your search term. This will initiate a search using the specified shortcut.
